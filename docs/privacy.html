GO HAM! Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

GO HAM! ("the game", "we") is a free arcade game. This page explains what information the game handles and why. We keep it to the minimum the game needs.

The short version: the game has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to put your score on the worldwide leaderboard.

What we collect

Only if you choose to join the worldwide leaderboard, we store:

  • A display name you type in (a first name and last initial, for example "Jessica C.").
  • A region you type in (such as a state, for example "TX").
  • Your best score on each level.
  • A random ID created on your device, used only to keep one leaderboard entry per player. It is not linked to your identity, phone number or any account, and it is not used for advertising.

If you tap Skip at the name prompt, nothing is sent to us. Your scores, coins and skins stay on your own device.

If you use Report a name on the leaderboard, we store the reported name and region and the random ID of the device that sent the report, so we can review it.

We do not collect your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise or GPS location, or any advertising identifier. The game contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no third-party analytics or tracking.

How it is used and who can see it

The information above exists only to run the in-game leaderboard. Your display name, region and score are shown publicly to other players on that leaderboard. Please do not enter a full name or anything you would not want other players to see. Names are filtered for offensive words and can be reported by other players.

Where it is stored

Leaderboard data is stored on our behalf by Supabase, a cloud database provider. We do not sell this information or share it with advertisers or any other third parties.

Information kept on your device

The game saves your progress (best scores, coins, unlocked skins and levels, the leaderboard name you chose, your settings, and any leaderboard names you have hidden) in your device's local storage. This never leaves your device and is erased if you delete the game or clear the site's data.

Children

GO HAM! is family friendly. The game can be played in full without entering any information: tap Skip at the name prompt. We do not knowingly collect contact information or precise location from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has added a leaderboard name you would like removed, email us and we will delete it.

Removing your information

To have your leaderboard entry deleted, email us with the name and region that were used and we will remove it, normally within a few days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change with it.
