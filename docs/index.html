Common questions

  • How do I play? Swipe left or right to change lanes, swipe up to jump (twice for a double jump), swipe down to roll, and tap to shoot corn. On a keyboard use the arrow keys, and F or Space to shoot.
  • How do I unlock the other worlds? Reach 50,000 points on Farm to open City, then 50,000 on City for Neon, then 50,000 on Neon for Space.
  • Does the game cost anything? No. There are no ads and nothing to buy. Skins are bought with coins you collect while playing.
  • How do I get on the worldwide leaderboard? After a run, type a first name, last initial and region. Your best score on each level is then posted automatically. Tap Skip if you would rather not appear, and nothing is sent.
  • How do I remove my name from the leaderboard? Email us the name and region you used and we will delete the entry.
  • I saw an inappropriate name. On the game-over screen, tap "Report a name" under the leaderboard, then REPORT next to it. It is hidden for you right away and sent to us for review. You can also email us.
  • My progress disappeared. Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the game or clearing browser data erases it.
